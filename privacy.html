Privacy Policy

Scam Check NZ

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. Our Commitment to Privacy

Scam Check NZ respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly in accordance with New Zealand's Privacy Act 2020.

2. Information We Process

When you submit a message, email, or website link for scam analysis, we process the information you provide.

Please remove passwords, banking details, verification codes, and other sensitive personal information before submitting content.

3. How We Use Your Information

Submitted information is sent to an artificial intelligence service provided by OpenAI to assess potential scam risks.

4. Storage and Retention

Scam Check NZ does not intentionally save submitted messages in its own database.

Our hosting and AI service providers may temporarily process or retain information for operational, security, or legal purposes under their applicable policies.

5. Third-Party Services

Scam Check NZ uses Render for website hosting and OpenAI for artificial intelligence processing. Information may be processed outside New Zealand.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Under New Zealand's Privacy Act 2020, you may have rights to request access to and correction of personal information held about you.

7. Contact Us

For privacy enquiries, please contact Scam Check NZ using the contact details that will be published before public launch.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services and practices change.
